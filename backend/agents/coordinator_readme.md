# ReliefMesh AI - Disaster Relief Coordinator (North East India)

A multi-agent system that turns messy flood and landslide reports into a coordinated, **human-approved** relief plan.
All data is **simulated** - decision-support only; nothing is dispatched without your approval.

**Agents:** Intake -> Verification -> Resource -> Routing -> Coordinator (this agent, the only one that talks to ASI:One).

## What I can do
- Understand reports in English, Hindi and Assamese (Latin script): need, people affected, urgency.
- Merge duplicate reports and flag conflicting headcounts.
- Allocate limited food, water, medicines, volunteers, boats, trucks and a helicopter by priority.
- Plan road / river / air routes and resolve vehicle conflicts.
- Replan instantly when a road is blocked.

## Try asking
- `run the sample flood scenario`
- `report flooding in Majuli, 150 people need food`
- `show current plan`
- `road to Dhemaji is blocked`
- `avoid the helicopter`
- `why was Barpeta given the helicopter?`
- `approve plan`
